## App name

Offline Translator

## Short description

Open-source offline voice and text translation, processed on your device

## Full description

Understand another language without relying on a cloud translator. Offline Translator is an open-source Android app for text translation and turn-based conversations on one phone.

OFFLINE AND ON YOUR DEVICE
Prepare your language resources online, then use the app without an internet connection. Speech recognition, translation and optional speech output use local models and prepared offline voices. No cloud translation service is needed.

CONVERSATION WITHOUT CONSTANT BUTTON PRESSES
Choose two languages and tap Start once. Then take turns speaking: the app recognizes speech, selects the translation direction between your chosen languages and translates each utterance automatically. There is no need to press a button before every phrase. Read translations on screen or enable spoken playback.

TYPE WHEN YOU PREFER
You can also type text to translate using the same language pair, without microphone access.

17 LANGUAGES
Chinese (Simplified Mandarin), Dutch, English, French, German, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Polish, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Thai, Turkish and Vietnamese. Speech availability depends on the selected resources and installed offline voices.

OPEN SOURCE
The app's source code is available to inspect, study and contribute to:
https://github.com/Ngoroth/offline-translator-android
The app's original code is licensed under Apache 2.0. Third-party components and models have their own licenses.

GROWING LANGUAGE SUPPORT
More languages are planned for future updates. The list above describes the current language catalog, not future availability.

BEFORE YOUR TRIP
Initial preparation requires Wi-Fi, large model downloads and sufficient free storage. Performance depends on your device. Conversation is turn-based, not simultaneous interpreting. Recognition and translation can make mistakes; check important details.
